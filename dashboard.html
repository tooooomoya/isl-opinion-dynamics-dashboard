network structure

same seed/step as the network snapshot above (undirected, giant-component-only for betweenness/λ2 — matches journal.tex's convention; degree distribution and clustering coefficient always describe the follow graph, the only one Java writes per-step CSVs for). Degree panels below plot the empirical CCDF P(X≥x) on log-log axes (Clauset/Newman/Shalizi convention); the dashed line, when shown, is the fitted power law for x≥xmin.
Echo-chamber diet (Cinelli et al. 2021): each node's own opinion (x) vs. the mean opinion of the accounts it follows (y). A tight positive diagonal = echo chambers (you mostly hear your own side); a shapeless blob = a well-mixed feed. Ringed = intervened hub.
