Network metrics

Degree

Follow-graph in-degree, out-degree, and total-degree distributions. Panels plot the empirical CCDF P(X≥x) on log-log axes; the dashed line, when shown, is the fitted power law for x≥xmin.

Topology / Connectivity

Clustering describes the follow graph. Betweenness and λ2 use its undirected giant component, matching the journal convention; giant-component fraction reports snapshot connectivity.

Polarization / Segregation

Snapshot controversy and opinion multimodality are shown with the selected run's modularity and E-I time series for the same shared Seed / Step context.

Exposure / Echo chamber

Echo-chamber diet (Cinelli et al. 2021): each node's own opinion (x) vs. the mean opinion of the accounts it follows (y). A tight positive diagonal = echo chambers (you mostly hear your own side); a shapeless blob = a well-mixed feed. Ringed = intervened hub.
