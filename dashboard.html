Dashboard Help & Reference

  

    
Welcome to the Opinion Dynamics Dashboard

    
This interactive dashboard displays live data from an opinion-dynamics simulation. It tracks how agent opinions, behavior metrics, and (if present) the social network co-evolve over time — auto-discovering whatever series and network attributes your model's run data provides.


    
💡 How to Use the Dashboard

    
    
      
  • Switching Tabs: Use the left sidebar to toggle between Time Series (general trend plots), Opinion/Outcome (opinions & phase plane), and Network (interactive graph visualization & metrics).
    • 
      
  • Interactive Zoom: Click and drag horizontally on any time-series chart to zoom in on a specific step range. Click the Reset Zoom button in the top header to reset.
    • 
      
  • Customizing Metric Cards: Use the "more metrics..." picker at the top to toggle charts on/off. Click the expand button (⤢) on any card to promote it to the larger featured row, or (⤡) to return it to the carousel.
    • 
      
  • Rewinding & Playing: In the Outcome Plane and Network views, drag the scrub sliders to travel back in time. Click Play (▶) to watch the historical progression of the network structure or outcome space.
    • 
      
  • Network Layout: In the Network Snapshot view, choose between standard Force or Gephi-style ForceAtlas2 layouts. You can tweak parameters (attraction, gravity, repulsion) and hit re-layout to adjust the visual structure.
