## 0. Scope & context

- **Goal:** extend WSSIT2026 from its minimal setting (top-3 hubs, one each at {−1,0,+1}, binary Active/Inactive on post+repost jointly) into a detailed analysis of how **environmental characteristics of moderate influencers (number × size/reach) and their repost-hub role** relate to overall polarization, with extreme (±1) influencers as a controlled background.
- **Out of scope:** `docs/journal.tex` (Structural Backfire Effect; single neutral hub gradually shifted to an extreme). Separate paper, same model. `repostNetwork` from that line is reused only as a measurement asset, not its narrative.
- **Three intervention designs coexist in the codebase** — do not conflate:
  1. WSSIT2026: top-3 hubs {−1,0,+1}, A/I on post+repost. ← base for this work.
  2. journal.tex: single neutral hub gradual shift (Δ=0.01). ← separate paper.
  3. `src/admin/ExpIntervention.java`: multi-tier (top-15), ratio-based `intrinsicOpinion` assignment. ← machinery usable for the count×size sweep.

## 1. Key mechanism (from code)

- Opinion update is Friedkin–Johnsen: `O = μ·O_intrinsic + (1−μ)·mean(feed)`, μ=0.8 (`Agent.updateMyself`).
- Repost propagation (`OpinionDynamics.java:176-184`): an agent reposts feed posts within its bounded confidence (bc); the reposted post keeps the **original author's opinion** and is broadcast to **all the reposter's followers**. The original author also receives a like → raises their posting probability (vocal-minority feedback).
- Consequence: a moderate repost hub's effect is **bc-dependent and bidirectional** —
  - small bc → only amplifies moderate content (depolarizing);
  - large bc → reposts both extremes → spreads extreme opinion to followers **and** boosts extreme authors' vocalness (polarizing / bridge).
- `repostNetwork[i][j]` already logs repost flows → usable to quantify a hub's cross-cutting amplification.

## 2. Methodology decision: randomize + regress (not fixed factorial, not univariate filter)

- Fixing {−1,0,+1} on top-3 (current driver) collapses the number/size axes → rejected for the main study.
- **Confounding caveat resolved:** the negative coupling between #moderate and #extreme hubs only bites under *fixed-budget composition analyzed univariately*. **Independent per-hub randomization + multivariate regression separates the main effects.**
- **Channel cannot be filtered** (it does not emerge) → it is an **assigned (randomized) treatment**; group by arm (an RCT). Number/size are observed covariates. → hybrid design, causally strong.
- **Per-run config logging is the lifeline:** each run must log, per intervened hub, ⟨hub_id, follower_count, tier, opinion_role, channel⟩ + outcome metrics + cross-cutting repost volume. Without it, post-hoc filtering/regression is impossible.
- Trial count scales with the **number of regression coefficients (~hundreds of runs)**, NOT the config space (4^K). Never need to cover the space.

## 3. Experiment decomposition (decouple to keep run counts tractable)

| Exp | Question | Design | ~runs |
|-----|----------|--------|-------|
| **0** | Are ±1 extremes the precondition? (WSSIT-style) | extreme activity sweep (incl. asymmetric), no moderate intervention | ~120 |
| **A** | Repost-hub role isolation | small fixed moderate hub set; channel ∈ {both, post-only, repost-only, none} × extreme{none, sym, asym} × seeds (CRN) | ~360 |
| **B** | Number × Size of moderate hubs | n_moderate ∈ {1,2,3,5} × tier ∈ {T1,T2,T3}; channel fixed; × extreme{none, sym, asym} × seeds | ~720 |

Total ≈ **1200 runs**.

### Extreme background factor — 3 levels (decided 2026-06-19, asymmetric added)
- **E0**: none (control).
- **E_sym**: {−1, +1} balanced pair.
- **E_asym**: one-sided (single extreme). Model is left/right symmetric (init `N(0,0.6)`), so run one direction and treat as representative.
- *(optional)* **E_imbal**: numerically imbalanced both-present, e.g. {−1,+1,+1}.

**Why extremes stay in A/B (not only Exp 0):** the headline WSSIT effect is an **interaction** (moderate hub matters *only when* opposing extremes present). The `E0` arm inside A/B is the built-in control proving the effect is conditional. Full extreme sweep belongs to Exp 0; A/B carry just the levels needed for the interaction.

## 4. Outcome metrics
- `postOpVar` (variance) = **bipolarization** — adequate for E0 / E_sym.
- **Asymmetric (E_asym) requires extra metrics:** one-sided *capture* shrinks variance even under strong manipulation. Add **mean shift `|E[O]|` / capture rate** toward the dominant pole, and **bimodality** (mode count / Hartigan dip) to separate "bridge keeps two modes" vs "capture collapses to one."
- Interpretation contrast enabled by E_asym: moderate repost hub as **two-sided bridge** (E_sym) vs **single-pole amplifier** (E_asym).

## 5. Variance reduction
- **CRN (common random numbers):** share network-generation seed across arms → far fewer seeds to detect channel/extreme contrasts via paired comparison.
- **Latin Hypercube** if continuous knobs are kept.
- **Two-stage:** coarse space-filling scan → dense runs only in the interesting region.

- **2026-06-23** — **Model-design critique for the moderate-influencer theme (CSS state-of-the-art review).** Identified five theme-specific weaknesses the current model must address before the "mid-scale moderate-but-vocal influencer paradoxically worsens polarization via cross-cutting reposting" claim is defensible:

Position/tolerance(ε)/bridging are entangled. **Critical:** at O=0 a ±1 post is "comfortable" only if ε≥1.0; Confidence Decline (ε←0.99 on any non-acceptable contact, tex L381) means the neutral hub can re-broadcast the extremes essentially only at t≈0. The paper's narrated bridge mechanism (L598-608) likely contradicts its own update rule — **must instrument code: count what/at-what-ε the neutral hub actually reposts.**
  2. **No commentary/quote-repost.** Repost is verbatim (conveyed opinion = O(p_j), tex L398-405); the "explainer/commentary" essence is absent, so the de-escalation counter-hypothesis is unrepresentable → a "moderate harmful" result is non-informative (equifinality).
  3. **Backfire is hardcoded.** Reposts restricted to comfortable posts (no out-group critique reposts); receiver is source-blind (no messenger/endorsement, no reactance) so contact→backfire is forced by asymmetric Confidence Decline. Backfire is empirically contested in 2026 (Guess & Coppock vs Bail 2018).
  4. **Scale not a factor + spiral-of-silence mutes moderates.** Hypothesis is about *mid-scale*, but leaders = top-3 in-degree (tex L424). Social Reinforcement (L345-366) drives low-comfortable-fraction (=balanced) agents' post prob to 0.05 → the "vocal moderate" is structurally silenced outside the 3 exogenous leaders.

- **2026-06-23** — **Reposter effect = relay distortion channel (DECIDED; simplifies the §3.1 λ-blend).** A repost no longer carries the original opinion verbatim. At emission in `repost()`, the conveyed opinion is reframed toward the reposter's own position: `O_conveyed = O_orig + s·(O_reposter − O_orig)`, `s ∈ [0,1]` (relay distortion strength). This is the §3.1 commentary blend with `s = 1−λ`: `s=0` = verbatim (the current behavior / ablation baseline), `s=1` = reposter fully overwrites. **Direction is endogenous to the reposter's position** — a moderate (O≈0) *mediates* extreme content toward center, an extremist (O≈±1) *extremizes* it — so moderation vs extremation emerges for free with no per-agent tag and no new state on `Post` (the reposter is `this` at emission). Implementation localized to `repost()` (return reframed `copyPost()`); downstream (`repostNetwork`, `receiveLike`, follower distribution) unchanged because the copy preserves the original `postUserId`. **`s` is a swept phase axis, not a constant.**
  - **Deliberately drops the "who" channel.** No reposter-identity envelope, no attribution change: `postUserId` stays = original author, so `unfollow()` still targets the source and conduit immunity is **untouched**. This isolates the *content-distortion* channel from the *attribution-bypass* channel (§3.2, not adopted) — a clean one-mechanism-at-a-time factorization. **Consequence (flagged):** Type 2 must now emerge from content distortion + open feed *alone*; the load shifts onto the feed system, which must stop the §2.2 self-limiting `unfollowList` filter or the fuel still burns out → Type 1.
  - **Coupled, non-optional companion change: allow reposting outside bounded confidence (DECIDED).** Distortion is near-inert under the current `|O_orig − O_self| < bc` repost gate (reposts are already within `bc`, so the gap `O_reposter − O_orig` is tiny and a moderate can never carry an extreme). So out-of-`bc` relay is permitted, but **as a named behavioral rule, not a deleted gate**: a separate `P_r^out` ("relay-as-explanation/dunk") channel, ideally distance/arousal-weighted. **Review framing (load-bearing):** BC is reframed as an *assimilation/affiliation* threshold (gates opinion update & follow), **not a sharing license** — sharing out-group content is empirically the *better-supported* default (Brady et al. 2017; Rathje, Van Bavel & van der Linden 2021; Berger & Milkman 2012), so this *corrects* a conflation rather than adding a DoF. Three guards against garden-of-forking-paths: ground in that literature pre-run, **sweep the gate as a factor** (gate-on/`s=0` baseline) rather than flipping it, pre-specify at the freeze gate. Keep `P_r^out` (entry) and `s` (transformation) doing provably distinct jobs to avoid the over-parameterization critique.
- **2026-06-23** — **Empirical grounding of Type 2 + a premise correction that reframes the repost channel (LITERATURE AUDIT).** Checked whether "controversial/affective polarization in a structurally *connected*, cross-cutting network" (Type 2) is empirically reported, vs structurally-separated polarization (Type 1).

  - **PREMISE CORRECTION (reviewer-catch):** the intuition "repost networks aren't segregated because of cross-cutting links" is **backwards**. Conover et al. 2011 — the **retweet network is sharply segregated** (retweet = endorsement → homophily); the **mention/reply network is the cross-cutting layer** (people @-mention out-group to argue/mock/quote-dunk). Cross-cutting links live in the *adversarial engagement* layer, not plain repost.
  - **Design implication (turns the correction into a strengthening):** frame the **out-of-`bc` `P_r^out` relay as the quote/critique/dunk layer, NOT ordinary endorsement-retweet.** Then the model inherits Conover's empirically-correct cross-cutting structure; ordinary in-`bc` reposts stay homophilous = the segregating Type-1 baseline pole; the moderate conduit = the empirically-attested **"bipartisan gatekeeper who pays a price"** (Garimella et al. 2018, WWW). Aligns with the already-logged §3.1 relay-distortion + out-of-BC decisions.
  - **Measurement consequence:** the follow/endorsement graph can be segregated *while* the reply/quote graph is cross-cutting **simultaneously** → compute the Type-1/Type-2 **structural-separation axis on the interaction graph the conduit actually feeds**, paired with affective bimodality over all `O_i`. Modularity on the follow graph alone can hide a live Type-2 state.
  - **Framing caveat:** backfire-via-contact is contested (Guess & Coppock 2020 vs Bail 2018) and echo-chamber strength is platform-dependent → keep the claim as a **phase diagram** ("where does the conduit flip harmful"), not "moderates are bad."
